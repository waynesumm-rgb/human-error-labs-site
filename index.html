02 / How we work

Keep it small.
Make it useful.
Then make it better.

Every tool, agent, and feature should earn its footprint. Start with what you have. Add complexity when the work calls for it.

  1. Define the problem

    Understand the current workflow, the constraints, and what a useful outcome looks like.

  2. Configure the solution

    Choose the right scope, tools, and AI roles. Set limits and decide where human review is needed.

  3. Build and test

    Build a focused version. Check its behavior against real examples, including the awkward ones.

  4. Refine with human oversight

    Review what works, correct what doesn’t, and let evidence guide the next iteration.

The name is intentional.

Humans have the ideas. Humans make mistakes. We build around both—using AI to extend what’s possible, while keeping people in charge.
